recon -- the enumeration surface (world-readable, published automatically
by this session's provisioning, no brute-forcing needed):
  ~/.halcyon/branch-protection.txt (published by 35-l17.sh) documents the
  model directly: refs/heads/main is fully protected (review + green CI +
  linear history, no direct push, no force push, no exemption at all),
  but ONE per-session exempt lane exists, deterministically chosen at
  gate time from three options (a `refs/tags/release-*` tag namespace, a
  `refs/heads/automerge/*` branch namespace, or a `refs/heads/hotfix/*`
  break-glass branch namespace -- picked via HMAC(secret,
  "<uid>:l17-path") % 3, varies by session). THIS session's box
  published:
    Exempt deploy lane (this environment): refs/heads/automerge/*

mechanism (from reading the repo's pre-receive hook):
  The pre-receive hook's exempt-lane check runs and short-circuits BEFORE
  the protected-ref check -- a push whose ref matches the `exempt-ref`
  glob in /run/dev/branch-rules is accepted without the review
  requirement, PROVIDED it's still a legal fast-forward (the lane skips
  review, not history-rewrite safety) and doesn't touch the one protected
  PATH (.halcyon-ci.yml, see level14). Critically, 35-l17.sh writes the
  `exempt-ref` and `deploy` directives with the IDENTICAL glob on purpose
  -- "the lane that skips review" and "the lane that ships" are the same
  ref, which is what makes L17 and L18 earnable from one push. On a legal
  accepted push through this lane, pre-receive emits the L17 flag
  directly over the push's stderr sideband, ONCE, only after confirming
  the WHOLE push was accepted.
